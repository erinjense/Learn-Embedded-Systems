# Books

The short shelf. Every book recommended anywhere in the guide, in one place, with who it is for. Free ones are marked. Links go to the author or publisher, never to a marketplace.

## Read these first

| Book | Who it is for | Free? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Making Embedded Systems, 2nd Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/making-embedded-systems/9781098151539/)** by Elecia White (2024) | Everyone. The one book to buy if you buy one. Architecture, drivers, debugging, testing, shipping. [Companion code](https://github.com/eleciawhite/making-embedded-systems). | No |
| **[Beej's Guide to C Programming](https://beej.us/guide/bgc/)** by Brian Hall | Learning C from scratch or relearning it properly. | Yes |
| **[Embedded C Coding Standard (BARR-C)](https://barrgroup.com/embedded-systems/books/embedded-c-coding-standard)** by Michael Barr | Anyone writing C that other people will read. Short. | Yes, PDF |
| **[Programming Embedded Systems](https://barrgroup.com/embedded-systems/books/programming-embedded-systems)** by Michael Barr and Anthony Massa | The classic introduction, now free chapter by chapter online. Dated examples, timeless structure. | Yes, online |

## C, deeper

| Book | Who it is for | Free? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Modern C](https://gustedt.gitlabpages.inria.fr/modern-c/)** by Jens Gustedt | Your second C book. Rigorous and current. | Yes, PDF |

## How computers work

| Book | Who it is for | Free? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Code: The Hidden Language of Computer Hardware and Software, 2nd ed.](https://www.charlespetzold.com/code/)** by Charles Petzold | High schoolers and anyone who wants the story from relays to CPUs. | No |
| **[Nand2Tetris](https://www.nand2tetris.org/)** | A course more than a book, but it comes with one. Build a computer from gates. | Yes, course |
| **[Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective](https://csapp.cs.cmu.edu/)** by Bryant and O'Hallaron | CS students who want the full systems picture: machine code, memory, linking, concurrency. | No |

## Firmware craft

| Book | Who it is for | Free? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Test-Driven Development for Embedded C](https://pragprog.com/titles/jgade/test-driven-development-for-embedded-c/)** by James Grenning | Anyone who thinks TDD does not apply to firmware. | No |
| **[Practical UML Statecharts in C/C++, 2nd ed.](https://www.state-machine.com/psicc2)** by Miro Samek | Hierarchical state machines and event-driven design, in depth. | Yes, PDF |
| **[Mastering the FreeRTOS Real Time Kernel](https://www.freertos.org/Documentation/02-Kernel/07-Books-and-manual/01-RTOS_book)** by Richard Barry | Using FreeRTOS. Official and current. | Yes |
| **[Bare-metal programming for ARM](https://github.com/umanovskis/baremetal-arm)** by Daniels Umanovskis | Startup code, linker scripts, and drivers from nothing, on an emulator. | Yes |
| **[Practical Guide to Bare Metal C++](https://alex-robenko.gitbook.io/bare_metal_cpp)** by Alex Robenko | If your team uses C++ on microcontrollers. | Yes |

| Book | Who it is for | Free? |
