# Phase 4 · Specializations

**Goal:** depth where your job or project needs it. Pick one or two. You do not need all six.

Each specialization is 2 to 4 weeks and assumes Phases 1 to 3.

| Module | Pick it if | Typical roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [12. Low power](12-low-power.md) | Anything runs on a battery. | Consumer products, wearables, IoT sensors, medical |
| [13. Robustness (ESD, EMI, EFT)](13-robustness.md) | Your product will be sold, which means it will be certified, which means it will be zapped in a lab. | Consumer, industrial, automotive, appliances |
| [14. Safety and security](14-safety-and-security.md) | A failure could hurt someone or a breach could hurt the company. | Automotive, medical, industrial, anything connected |
| [15. Embedded Linux](15-embedded-linux.md) | The product has a screen, a network stack, or a camera, and a microcontroller is not enough. | Gateways, HMIs, robotics, set-top and infotainment |
| [16. Wireless](16-wireless.md) | It talks to a phone, a cloud, or other devices without a cable. | IoT, consumer, smart home, asset tracking |
| [17. Signals, control, motors](17-dsp-control-motors.md) | It measures, filters, or moves something physical. | Audio, robotics, drones, appliances, power electronics |

> **Which one first?**
>
> If you work on consumer products, do 12 and 13. They are the two that most surprise new engineers, and they are rarely taught in school.
